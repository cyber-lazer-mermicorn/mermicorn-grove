## Quality contract summary

- Easy exhibits: teach one concept, parse/compile/execute, deterministic, safe
- Advanced exhibits: W4H rationale, typed I/O, validation, invariants, observability, runnable demo
- No placeholders, no false success, no unconditional pass
- Evidence gates: structural ≠ compiler proof ≠ production proof
